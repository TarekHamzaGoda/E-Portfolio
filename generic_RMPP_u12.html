Unit 12

Individual Reflective Piece

What?

Going through this module, I engaged in a series of assignments and activities that allowed me to develop a robust foundation in research methodology, data analysis, and professional development. In that sense, it was evident that the module’s aim is to essentially prepare the students for the final dissertation project.

However, my main concern at the start of this module was my uncertainty of the skillset that was required in order to explore the academic landscape relevant to my research question, evaluating, and synthesizing key sources. And even though I have encountered numerous literature reviews and research papers, I lacked the fundamental skills to create them. Luckly, among the most significant components during this module were the literature review and the research proposal presentation, which provided the opportunity to apply theoretical knowledge in a practical academic context.

The literature review, for instance, has sharpened my ability to conduct systematic searches, assess the credibility of academic sources, and organise them into coherent themes. Similarly, the research presentation proposal that was focused on “real-time operational dashboards in supply chain management” has encouraged me to define a clear research question, justify methodological choices, anticipate challenges, and articulate ethical considerations. These tasks involved not only critical thinking but also clarity of communication which is an essential skill for any researcher (Bryman, 2016).

Nonetheless, the supporting units in this module ensured that I gain technical competencies in my research practices by providing guidelines such as research methods, approaches, ethical responsibilities and even statistical analysis. Most of which were essential for the success of both assignments and are supplemental for the upcoming dissertation project.

So What?

One of the most challenging parts of the literature review that I encountered was the selection between large number of sources to find arguments that specifically supported or critiqued the core research question: How does RTOD affect operational performance? It required both patience and critical thinking to identify studies that not only aligned with the topic but also added depth to the conversation. However, by incorporating these findings in a balanced way, I was able to critically showcase both benefits and limitations of RTOD’s adoption. Such as experiences have had a tangible impact on both my academic development and my confidence as a researcher. By conducting the literature review, I was challenged to consider not just what the literature said but why it mattered, how it informed my research direction, and how my work could potentially contribute.

Similarly, I recognized that I need to improve in highlighting both the strengths and limitations of my research. For instance, in my research proposal presentation, while I effectively showcased the benefits of RTOD, such as visibility and decision-making speed, I could have more clearly discussed where the literature was inconclusive or highlighted studies that challenged RTOD adoption altogether. That is because developing this level of critique will help me produce more balanced and rigorous academic work. That said, this assignment showcased the importance of designing research with real-world relevance. My topic on RTODs required me to bridge theory and practice. I had to think critically about data collection, including qualitative interviews, case studies, surveys, and the role of mixed-methods approaches. Mixed-methods research is especially valuable when trying to uncover both measurable outcomes and human experiences, which aligns well with my aim to assess operational performance and industry perception (Creswell & Creswell, 2018).

Additionally, the data analysis units were especially valuable in shaping my understanding of how to approach quantitative research. This was directly useful when planning how to measure KPIs like cost efficiency and decision-making speed in my proposal. However, hypothesis testing was particularly challenging. Concepts like null and alternative hypotheses, paired versus independent t-tests, and interpreting p-values were initially overwhelming. Thankfully, working with descriptive statistics excel exercises has reinforce my understanding of statistical reasoning, especially when drawing conclusions from numerical data. With more practice and perhaps supplemental learning in statistics, I hope to gain more confidence in applying inferential statistics to real-world research scenarios.

Finally, another key outcome has been the increased awareness of ethical responsibilities in research. Considering GDPR compliance, participant consent, and data anonymization during the proposal helped me understand how ethics must be embedded at every stage of a study. Ethical principles such as confidentiality, informed consent, and data protection are not only regulatory requirements but also essential for maintaining research integrity (Resnik, 2020).

Now What?

Moving forward, I intend to build on the foundational skills developed throughout this module by deepening my capabilities in academic research and data analysis. One clear area for growth is my ability to critically evaluate literature, particularly in highlighting the strengths and limitations of sources, as I found this challenging when aligning studies with my research question on RTOD. I will also focus on reinforcing my understanding of statistical concepts such as hypothesis testing, which initially proved difficult despite completing the practical exercises. To support this, I plan to supplement my learning with targeted resources and begin applying these techniques to real datasets more regularly. Alongside this, I aim to enhance my use of data tools like Power BI and Tableau, which are directly relevant to my research and professional aspirations in supply chain and operations analytics.

Assuming I continue pursuing the same research question “How does RTOD affect operational performance?” this module has laid a solid foundation for my final dissertation. The work done on the research proposal can evolve into a full research project, supported by refined methodologies and more advanced data collection strategies. I now have a clear understanding of how to integrate ethical research practices, align mixed-methods designs with real-world industry needs, and communicate research findings in both academic and applied contexts. These competencies will be instrumental not just for completing my dissertation, but also for my continued development as a data-informed researcher and professional.
